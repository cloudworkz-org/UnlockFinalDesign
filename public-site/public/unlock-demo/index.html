Why Unlock exists

Managing your own investments has always meant making your own decisions. Over the past ten years those decisions have become harder: pension, tax and inheritance rules now affect each other, they keep changing, and joining them up is left to you.

Unlock provides structured decision support for High Net Worth and self-certified sophisticated investors who stay responsible for their own financial position. It gives you one view of all your assets: regulated investments such as stocks, pensions and bonds, as well as unregulated investments such as property, gold and crypto. That view helps you make more informed decisions and see their effect across areas such as tax and retirement, with the sources, assumptions, uncertainty and next step always in view.

Five things you will see.

Together we choose one of several example investors, such as a couple nearing retirement, a founder after selling a business, or a property landlord, and follow them through Unlock. It all uses sample information.

  1. 01

    Your whole picture

    Everything the example investor owns in one place, from pensions and investments to property and the harder-to-value things, set up around their reasons for using Unlock. Every page has Learn and Ask, for an explanation or a question about what is on screen.

  2. 02

    How it holds up

    How that mix of holdings has behaved in past good and bad times, including the worst and best cases, and how it compares with other portfolios.

  3. 03

    The impact of EIS

    What an EIS programme could look like as a range of outcomes rather than a promise, and how a company's claims stand up against the evidence: what is supported, what is still open, and the questions to ask.

  4. 04

    Income for life

    What those assets could provide year by year in retirement, with everything included.

  5. 05

    For the people closest to you

    Heartbeat. If you don't log in for a set period, Unlock contacts the people you have chosen, so they can find your financial picture and where everything is. Our founding investors asked for it.
